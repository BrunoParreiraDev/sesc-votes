## Reporting a Vulnerability

Use this section to guide people on how to report a vulnerability in your project.

If someone discovers a security issue, they should:

- **Contact Information**: Provide clear information on where and how to report vulnerabilities. This could include an email address, a link to a form, or a dedicated issue tracker.

- **Expectations for Reporting**: Inform them about how often they can expect updates after reporting a vulnerability.

- **Handling of Vulnerability Reports**: Outline what happens after someone reports a vulnerability. For example:
  - How long it might take for a response.
  - The process for evaluating the reported vulnerability.
  - What to expect in case the vulnerability is accepted or declined.
  
- **Responsible Disclosure**: Encourage responsible disclosure and ask reporters not to disclose the vulnerability publicly until it has been resolved or a fix has been provided.

- **Appreciation**: Express appreciation for responsible reporting and their efforts in making the project more secure.

Please make sure that the reporting guidelines are clear and concise to encourage responsible reporting and ensure the security of your project.
